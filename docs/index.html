Solar Flare Forecast

This page shows a live daily forecast of whether a major (M or X class) solar flare will happen on the Sun in the next 24 hours. It updates on its own once a day, logs every forecast, and grades itself against what actually happened and against NOAA's official forecast. I built it to learn how this works, and the whole point was to keep it honest. The code and the full writeup are on GitHub.

Active-region data comes from NASA and Stanford JSOC, flare outcomes and the NOAA baseline from the NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center, and the training data from the SWAN-SF benchmark. On held out historical data the forecast scores an AUC of 0.95. On the live Sun it has not shown that skill yet, and the scoreboard above is the real number. For a while the two were not even measured the same way, which I found in September 2026 and wrote up as Step 14 in EXPLAINED.md.
